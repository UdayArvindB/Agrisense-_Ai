# Title: Regenerative Living Soil & Microbial Health Standards
# Category: Soil Management
# Source: Bureau of Agro-Ecosystems & Soil Health
# Year: 2026

## Fostering Systemic Acquired Resistance (SAR)
Living soils enriched with diverse microbial consortia and high soil organic carbon (SOC > 1.2%) stimulate systemic plant defense pathways. Beneficial mycorrhizal fungi (Glomus intraradices) and plant-growth-promoting rhizobacteria (Pseudomonas fluorescens) colonize root surfaces, physically shielding plants from soil-borne pathogens like Fusarium and Verticillium.

## Actionable Soil Protocols
1. Cover Cropping: Integrate legume green manure crops (such as Sunn Hemp or Sesbania) during inter-season periods to fix atmospheric nitrogen and enhance aggregate soil porosity.
2. Balanced N-P-K & Micronutrient Supply: High vegetative nitrogen fertilization promotes soft, succulent cellular tissue vulnerable to foliar blights. Maintain a potassium-to-nitrogen ratio of 1.4:1 during fruit enlargement to fortify epidermal cuticle thickness.
3. Compost Inoculation: Apply mature, aerobic compost at 5 to 8 tons per hectare annually to increase cation-exchange capacity and water-holding capacity.
