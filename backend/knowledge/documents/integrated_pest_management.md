# Category: Pest Management

## Managing Foliar Disease Vectors
Sucking insect pests—predominantly whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci), thrips, and aphids—act as primary biological vectors for severe viral diseases including Tomato Leaf Curl Virus and Spotted Wilt Virus. Furthermore, mechanical rasping injuries created by thrips provide direct entry points for secondary fungal spore germ tubes.

## Integrated Biocontrol Measures
1. Monitoring & Trapping: Deploy 25 to 30 yellow sticky traps per hectare at crop canopy level for whiteflies and aphids, and blue sticky cards for thrips. Inspect weekly to determine threshold levels.
2. Botanical Sprays: Cold-pressed neem oil (Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm) applied at 2 to 3 ml per liter of water acts as an oviposition deterrent and insect growth regulator.
3. Beneficial Insects: Conserve and introduce predatory green lacewings (Chrysoperla carnea) and parasitic wasps (Encarsia formosa) to naturally regulate nymph populations without broad-spectrum chemical knockdowns.
