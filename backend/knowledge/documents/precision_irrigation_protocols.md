# Title: Precision Micro-Irrigation & Canopy Moisture Protocols
# Category: Irrigation
# Source: National Water Conservation & Precision Agronomy Station
# Year: 2026

## Impact of Irrigation on Foliar Pathogens
Overhead sprinkler irrigation systems significantly elevate the duration of leaf wetness and cause splash transmission of fungal conidia and bacterial cells. Subsurface and surface drip micro-irrigation systems deliver moisture directly into root profiles while keeping leaves completely dry.

## Best Management Practices
1. Timing: Schedule drip irrigation pulses during early dawn hours. This enables ambient heat and morning wind to rapidly evaporate any incidental humidity.
2. Soil Moisture Tension: Maintain tensiometer readings between -25 kPa and -35 kPa in the active root horizon (15 cm - 30 cm depth) for vegetable crops.
3. Mulch Integration: Combine drip lines with organic straw or reflective polyethylene mulch film to suppress weed transpiration and reduce soil water evaporation by up to 40%.
4. Acid Flushing: Periodically flush drip lines with mild phosphoric or citric acid solutions to prevent calcium carbonate emitter plugging.
